Where this comes from

The shape

Traced row-by-row off seven product photographs and the dimensions printed on the spec card, then lofted as superellipse cross-sections. Not tooling-accurate — good for renders, comps, and exactly this kind of showcase. See the reconstruction's own MEASUREMENTS.md for the full accuracy breakdown.

The colors

Sampled as exact pixel values from the bev. brand book's color palette and primary logo pages, not eyeballed. Hydrate, Perform and Recover are the three colorways the brand book itself names; Berry and Signature are honest extensions built from its secondary swatches and wordmark ink, labeled as such rather than presented as official brand pillars.
